Cookie Policy

Transparent disclosure of web cookies, analytics identifiers, and browser preference management at RibKnitCairn.

1. Definition & Purpose of Cookies

RibKnitCairn utilizes web cookies, local storage tokens, and comparable tracking mechanisms to deliver essential website functionality and analyze aggregate visitor traffic patterns. Cookies are miniature text files placed upon your local browsing hardware that allow our servers to recognize returning visitors, store shopping preferences, and maintain seamless checkout workflows. This policy clarifies our cookie deployment methods and details your administrative controls.

2. Essential System Cookies

Essential cookies are strictly necessary to enable core website navigation, enforce HTTPS encryption protocols, and support interactive basket operations. Without these fundamental system cookies, basic web services such as custom sock sizing selections and secure transactional checkouts cannot function reliably. Because these operational files are essential to deliver requested digital services, they do not require optional prior consent under global electronic privacy frameworks.

3. Analytical Telemetry & Performance Metrics

Analytical and performance cookies collect aggregate, anonymized metrics regarding how visitors discover and traverse our technical sock articles and product catalogs. We utilize Google Analytics (identifier G-0LY0HY7L01) to evaluate page response times, monitor geographic traffic trends, and identify potential navigation bottlenecks. These insights empower our engineering team to improve catalog layout, enhance mobile interface speeds, and maintain stable server uptime.

4. User Preference Controls & Browser Settings

We respect your digital autonomy and provide clear mechanisms to regulate cookie storage preferences across your desktop and mobile browsing devices. You may configure your internet browser settings to decline all optional cookies, notify you when files are transmitted, or delete previously stored browser cookies. Please be advised that disabling specific functional cookies may limit your ability to utilize certain interactive features within our online store.

5. Technical Inquiries & Governance Desk

For detailed inquiries concerning our cookie governance procedures or to adjust your recorded preferences, contact our technical privacy coordinator directly. Written correspondence should be addressed to RibKnitCairn Woolen Co., 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States, or communicated via telephone at +1-888-777-5845. We review our digital tracking architecture regularly to ensure ongoing alignment with evolving international privacy statutes.
