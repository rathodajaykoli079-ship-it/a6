# RibKnitCairn Design Registry & Architecture

## 1. Brand Philosophy & Identity
- **Brand Name:** RibKnitCairn
- **Niche:** Highland Heritage Rib-Knit Merino Wool Socks, Alpine Boot Liners & Trail Footwear
- **Inspiration:** Stone cairns guiding alpine travelers across mountain ridges; structural rib-knits that deflect friction and cushion impact.

## 2. Color Palette & Semantics
- **Deep Earth Cairn Brown:** `#3d2b1f` / Dark `#271a12` (Primary Brand & Navigation)
- **Terracotta Heather / Cairn Marker:** `#c46d3b` / Hover `#ad5c2e` (Accent, Action CTAs & Markers)
- **Highland Slate Blue:** `#2f4858` (Secondary Depth & Telemetry Accents)
- **Oatmeal Fleece Off-White:** `#faf7f2` (Primary Background)
- **Clean Surface White:** `#ffffff` (Card & Panel Surface)
- **Muted Stone Slate:** `#78716c` / `#57534e` (Body Copy & Secondary Details)
- **Stone Border Line:** `#e7dfd5` (Subtle Dividers)

## 3. Typography Hierarchy
- **Primary Display & Headings:** `Cinzel`, serif (Weights: 600, 700, 800)
- **Body & Interface:** `Plus Jakarta Sans`, sans-serif (Weights: 400, 500, 600, 700)
- **Technical Telemetry & Badges:** `Space Grotesk`, monospace (Weights: 500, 600)

## 4. Header & Navigation Structure
- Strictly 1 `<header class="site-header">` per page with backdrop-filter blur.
- Strictly 1 `<div class="mobile-drawer" id="mobile-drawer">` per page synchronized in `assets/js/main.js`.
- Escape key and overlay dismissal support.
