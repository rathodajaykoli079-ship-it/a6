Privacy Policy

Comprehensive disclosure regarding data protection, customer record retention, and algorithmic transparency at RibKnitCairn Woolen Co.

1. Institutional Scope & Commitment

RibKnitCairn operates this institutional portal to present our master highland merino wool socks, technical footwear research, and artisan knitting services. We collect limited digital analytics and customer communication records strictly to process trail orders, respond to sizing inquiries, and optimize user experience across our digital catalog. We adhere to rigorous global data security benchmarks and protect your privacy against unauthorized extraction or dissemination across global commercial networks.

2. Categories of Information Collected

When you browse our online collections, request custom wool socks, or sign up for our seasonal dispatch journal, we may collect technical identifiers including Internet Protocol addresses, browser agent specifications, and navigation timestamps. For direct orders or atelier fittings, we collect your name, shipping address, contact phone, and payment details through encrypted processing systems. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to marketing aggregators or commercial syndicates.

3. Lawful Basis for Data Processing

Our processing activities rest upon legitimate business execution, contractual fulfillment for delivered wool socks, and explicit user consent for informational newsletters. We retain customer transaction records solely for the duration mandated by financial accounting regulations and tax auditing standards. Non-identifying browsing telemetry is reviewed periodically to eliminate website navigation bottlenecks and optimize mobile responsiveness across handheld outdoor navigation devices on mountain trails worldwide.

4. Security Safeguards & Data Transmission

We implement robust cryptographic safeguards including Secure Sockets Layer protocols, enterprise firewalls, and restricted credentials to shield your sensitive information. Access to transactional records is limited strictly to trained personnel who manage fulfillment logistics, sizing consultations, and warranty servicing. Despite sophisticated security controls, digital communications involve inherent risks, and users are encouraged to maintain confidential credentials at all times during online sessions.

5. Individual Rights & Regulatory Compliance

Depending upon your geographic jurisdiction, you hold specific statutory rights regarding your stored personal data, including the right to inspect, correct, export, or request deletion of your records. You may also object to automated data processing or revoke consent for informational marketing dispatches at any time without penalty. To exercise these individual privacy rights, submit a written inquiry directly to our data governance desk at our primary postal atelier location.

6. Privacy Desk & Contact Information

Our highland atelier is committed to resolving all customer data questions with transparency and professional diligence. Please address your formal privacy requests to Data Compliance Officer, RibKnitCairn Woolen Co., 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States, or reach our telephone support desk directly at +1-888-777-5845. We review and update this privacy policy periodically to reflect evolving data protection statutes and advanced technological benchmarks.
